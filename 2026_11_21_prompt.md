# Taco Games — Update Roadmap

## Launcher & System Updates

* **Currency Overhaul:** Rename the platform-wide currency from **Goons** to **Tacos** across all menus, shops, and games.

* **External Games Tab:** Add a dedicated **"External Games"** tab in the launcher to organize and showcase games created by 3rd parties and imported into the platform.

* **Livestreaming Fixes & Controls:**

  * Fix the bug causing livestreams to endlessly display `"Connecting to game..."`.

  * Add a clear, functional **"Leave"** button to exit active livestreams smoothly.

## New Full Games

* **Sketch:** A vertical platformer inspired by *Doodle Jump*.

* **Zombie vs. You:** A fast-paced zombie invasion escape game.

* **DoomsDay:** An action-packed shooter game.

* **Edible Light Bulb Clicker:** An incremental clicker game inspired by *Cookie Clicker*, themed entirely around edible light bulbs.

* **Crochet/Knitting Simulator:** A cozy crafting simulator where you crochet and knit various items.

* **World Conflict:** A grand strategy simulation inspired by *Ages of Conflict*, set on a 3D globe with significantly deeper mechanics and detail.

* **Slither:** A competitive arena game based on *Snake.io*.

* **Design Anything:** A creative sandbox allowing you to design custom creations from a massive list of options and templates.

* **Bing Bong BANG!:** A multi-level rhythm game.

* **Pancake Simulator:** Play and experience life as a pancake.

## New Minigames

* **Rock Simulator:** Features two distinct modes:

  * *Regular Mode:* First-person perspective where you cannot move, only look around a still environment.

  * *Realistic Mode:* Completely black screen for the true rock experience.

* **Battleship:** Classic naval strategy added to the minigame lineup.

## Existing Game Updates & Improvements

* **Tic Tac Toe:** Add **Ultimate Tic Tac Toe** as a playable mode option.

* **Imposter: Shades in the Lighthouse:** Add a toggle option when playing in all-bot lobbies to **force yourself to be the Impostor**.

* **Sports Mix:** Overhaul and improve the **Bowling** mechanics and overall feel.

* **Chess & Checkers:** Add a **Material Counter** UI feature so players can easily track their captured pieces and material advantage during a match.
