Rules of Play

Goal: Buy properties, collect rent, build houses/hotels, and avoid bankruptcy!

Turn Flow:

  • Roll the dice to advance around the 40-space board.
  • If you roll doubles, you get another turn! Rolling doubles 3 times in a row sends you directly to Jail.
  • Land on an unowned property to buy it or pass.
  • Land on an owned property to pay rent to the owner.

Building Houses: When you own all properties of a single color set, you can build houses to increase rent exponentially!

Jail Mechanics: Exit jail by paying $50, rolling doubles, or waiting up to 3 turns.
